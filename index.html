Virtual Camera Setup

Use Speculum Visus as a webcam in Zoom, Teams, Meet, and more.

1
Open OBS Studio Free and open source. Download at obsproject.com if you don't have it.
2
Add a Window Capture source In the Sources panel, click + → Window Capture, then select this browser window.
3
Start Virtual Camera Click Start Virtual Camera in the Controls panel (bottom right in OBS).
4
Select it in your video app In Zoom, Teams, Meet etc., choose OBS Virtual Camera as your camera source.
Tip: Press H to hide the lens picker strip before switching to OBS for a clean full-frame capture. Press H again to bring it back.

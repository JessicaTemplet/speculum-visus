Privacy Policy

Speculum Visus — speculumvisus.templetsolutions.com

Speculum Visus is a personal, browser-based application that uses the Snap Camera Kit SDK to apply augmented reality lenses to your live camera feed. This policy explains what data is accessed, what is collected, and by whom.

Camera Access

Speculum Visus requests access to your device camera solely to render AR lens effects in real time. Your camera feed is processed entirely within your browser. No video, images, or frames are recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server operated by this application.

Data Collected by This App

This application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts are created, no usage data is logged, and no analytics are run by Speculum Visus itself.

Snap Camera Kit SDK

This application is built on the Snap Camera Kit SDK, provided by Snap Inc. As part of normal SDK operation, Snap may collect anonymized usage and performance metrics from Camera Kit integrations. This data collection is governed by Snap's own privacy practices.

For details on what Snap collects through Camera Kit, please review:

• Snap Privacy Policy
• Snap Camera Kit Terms of Service

No Third-Party Advertising

LensCam does not serve ads, does not share data with advertisers, and does not use any third-party analytics or tracking services beyond what the Snap Camera Kit SDK itself may collect as described above.

Cookies and Local Storage

LensCam does not use cookies or local storage. No data is persisted between sessions.

Children's Privacy

This application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. The effective date at the bottom of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of LensCam after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
